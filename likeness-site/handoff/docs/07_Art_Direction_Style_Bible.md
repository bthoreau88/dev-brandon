# OVS Art Direction Style Bible

## Global Rule
The game and web companion must look like one living OVS universe. No generic horror. No generic tech demo. No plastic AI sheen.

## Palette
- Near-black charcoal.
- Motel tungsten amber.
- Sourceless teal / green-cyan shadow.
- Muted olive metadata.
- Cream/off-white paper for documents.
- Distressed brass / oxidized gold.
- Warm brown skin preserved.

## Cinematography
- Controlled, patient, witness-like camera.
- Mid-conversation tension.
- Naturalistic lens behavior.
- Subtle anamorphic response where appropriate.
- Negative fill and motivated practical light.

## UI
- Sparse.
- Editorial.
- OVS-style uppercase labels.
- Thin divider lines.
- Cream/black/muted olive palette.
- No sci-fi HUD unless diegetic.

## 3D Material Language
- Worn motel paint.
- Stained carpet fiber.
- Dust and fingerprints.
- Brass scratches.
- Wet glass.
- Real fabric weave.
- Skin pores and natural asymmetry.

## Forbidden Drift
- Cartoon unless explicitly assigned to a stylized project branch.
- Superhero pose language.
- Overlit cinematic blue.
- Generic haunted house props.
- Gore as a substitute for dread.
- Glossy plastic faces.
- Random cyberpunk neon.
