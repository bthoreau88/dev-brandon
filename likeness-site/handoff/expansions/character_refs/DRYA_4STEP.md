# DRYA — 4-Step Reference Pack (gate before ANY 3D)

`CHR_DRYA_001`. Run in order. Model is built from the sheets that pass QC, never from
loose art. Identity lock is law. **Drya dignity lock:** she is a counterpart and witness
with emotional force — never decorative suffering, never a glamour model.

**IDENTITY LOCK (must hold):** Black woman, late 20s, warm medium-brown skin, **dancer
body language**, **jewelry / piercing detail**, guarded emotional force.
**Drift risk to reject:** generic glamour model, body-type drift, victim-only staging.

---

## Step 1 · IDEATION — lock concept, silhouette, mood
> Cinematic character ideation, DRYA — a late-20s Black woman, the relationship anchor
> and contradiction source in a motel-noir psychological horror. Warm medium-brown skin,
> dancer's carriage, jewelry and a single locket, deep olive/black wardrobe. Mood:
> composed, grieving without spectacle, watchful. 6–12 variants exploring silhouette and
> presence. ARRI-realism, restrained, full dignity.
> **Do NOT yet:** T-pose, white background, orthographic accuracy, glamour styling.
> **Output:** selected concept + identity-lock statement.

## Step 2 · CLEAN SHEET — modeling reference
> Single full-body character sheet of DRYA, neutral A-pose, facing camera, **flat even
> neutral lighting, plain neutral background**, no distortion, no grade. Real skin
> texture. Wardrobe locked: deep olive/black layers, dancer's posture, jewelry +
> locket. Fuller, grounded body type — no slimming drift.
> **Do NOT:** poster lighting, glamour smoothing, suggestive staging.
> **Output:** one clean full-body hero sheet (the modeling master).

## Step 3 · PARTS — protect the high-risk details
- `DRYA_PART_face` — front + 3/4, accurate features, restraint.
- `DRYA_PART_jewelry` — earrings / piercings, exact.
- `DRYA_PART_locket` — the tintype locket (also prop `PROP_LOCKET_001`).
- `DRYA_PART_hands` — dancer's hands, neutral.
- `DRYA_PART_wardrobe` — olive/black layer, fabric, drape.
> **Do NOT:** redesign parts, change materials, or alter body type between sheets.
> **Output:** part sheets with IDs.

## Step 4 · VIEWS — the modeling blueprint
> Orthographic turnaround of DRYA in the locked wardrobe: **front, left side, back,
> three-quarter**. Identical proportions across all views, neutral light/background,
> no perspective.
> **Acceptance:** recognizable in silhouette, face, wardrobe, jewelry from front + side,
> or **NOT READY**.
> **Output:** orthographic view pack → Blender/3D (see `../blender_fal/`).

---

## QC gate before promotion
Identity holds · dignity lock held (no victim-only / glamour drift) · body type
consistent · jewelry + locket correct · neutral light/background on sheets · proportions
consistent · materials named. Stage in `/expansions/_staging/character_refs/drya/`, QC,
then `_approved/`.
